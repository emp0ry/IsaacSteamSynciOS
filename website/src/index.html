THE DETAILS

A journey between devices
With a verified return.

Save progression, Continue data, local backups and additive achievements, each handled for its own purpose.

01

Connect directly.

Sign in within the native app using Steam Mobile QR or credentials and Steam Guard. Reconnect through the Keychain. Traffic goes directly to Valve.

No desktop client, companion app or developer-hosted sync server.

02

Move a complete save.

Compare local and remote state against the verified baseline. Pull remote-only changes, upload stable local-only progress, and choose explicitly for a conflict.

First sync and conflicting saves never guess a field-by-field merge.

03

Preserve what you earned.

Versioned local and remote backups, read-back verification, and restore. Manual Sync Now adds missing save-derived achievements to Steam without clearing unlocks.

Progression sync and additive achievement sync are distinct operations.

  1. Install the package.

    Use the released DEB in a compatible rootless ElleKit setup, then fully restart Isaac.

  2. Connect Steam in the app.

    Complete your Steam and Guard flow in the native interface.

  3. Choose your starting save.

  1. Use the native library.

    Embed the released ARM64 dylib into your own compatible decrypted Isaac app.

  2. Patch and sign correctly.

    The documented patch-ipa.sh tool produces unsigned output unless a signing identity is supplied. Follow the current README.

  3. Restart and connect.

    Launch the correctly signed app and connect Steam through the native interface.

Save and Exit before switching devices. A live in-memory room is not transferred. Pull or restore after gameplay begins waits for a relaunch.

A LITTLE MORE CONTEXT

Good to know.

Are conflicting saves combined?+

No. Conflicts require a deliberate choice of one complete save. Achievement additions are handled separately and never clear Steam unlocks.

Can I start while offline?+

Yes. Startup network waiting is bounded to eight seconds. Background execution is also bounded by iOS.

Where do I enter my Steam account?+

Only in the native application’s Steam flow. This website never requests credentials or performs account authentication.
